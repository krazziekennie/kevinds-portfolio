02

The Designer

Issue No. 01 — the story of someone who refused to pick a side.

I'm KEVINDS — a Designer exploring where creativity meets artificial intelligence.

It started with curiosity — a fascination with how ideas could be transformed into experiences people can see, feel, and connect with. Over time, that curiosity led me into the world of design, branding, visual storytelling, motion, and digital experiences.

Today, design is at the center of everything I do.

As a Graphic Designer & Video Editor at Rocktfuel Agency, I create visual identities, social media campaigns, restaurant menus, brand creatives, AI-generated visuals, and video content for real brands. From choosing the right typography and building strong compositions to creating visuals that capture attention, I enjoy turning simple ideas into work that feels bold, meaningful, and memorable.

My background in AI & Data Science gives me a different perspective on the creative process. While design is my primary focus, technology has become one of my strongest creative tools. I explore AI to generate new visual possibilities, experiment with ideas faster, and push creative boundaries beyond traditional workflows.

I don't see design and technology as separate worlds. For me, the best creative work happens when strong ideas, human emotion, visual design, and intelligent tools come together.

Whether it's building a brand identity, designing a campaign, editing a video, or experimenting with AI-generated visuals, my goal is always the same: Create work that doesn't just look good — but feels right.

Still learning. Still building. Still experimenting. This is only Issue No. 01.
